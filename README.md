# RACELEDGER

RaceLedger is a motorsport financial intelligence platform which is designed to connect with vehicle performance, 
operational expenditure, component's lifecycle management and financial forecasting under one roof (single website).

As of first version, We are going to have a Silverstone circuit simulation with an F1 car onboard and let you know both the car details while on traack and every decisions taken
pre-race, during race and post-race time.
